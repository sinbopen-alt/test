Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This is a private, non-commercial tool used by its owner to retrieve and review the owner's own WHOOP data.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit authorization, the tool may access WHOOP recovery, cycle, sleep, workout, profile, and body-measurement data.

Purpose and storage

Data is used only for the owner's personal health tracking and analysis. Retrieved data and authorization credentials are stored locally on the owner's personal computer. This tool does not operate a remote database.

Sharing and sale

The tool does not sell, advertise with, or intentionally share WHOOP data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention and may delete the locally stored data and credentials at any time. WHOOP access can also be revoked through the owner's WHOOP account or developer application settings.

Changes

This notice may be updated if the tool's use of data changes.
